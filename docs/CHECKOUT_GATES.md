# Commission checkout go-live gates — CURRENT STATUS: NOT ACTIVE

RelayMarket implements a noncustodial EIP-5792 atomic smart-wallet payment architecture. An operational independent seller payout wallet and owner treasury are BOTH required before the public payment button is enabled.

## Already implemented in source
- Canonical Circle native Base USDC token and Base chain ID 8453 pinned.
- 1000 bps marketplace commission / 9000 bps seller payout, BigInt six-decimal precise calculation.
- User-controlled smart-wallet wallet_sendCalls, atomicRequired=true; no sequential fallback.
- wallet_getCapabilities atomic readiness check and independent Base mainnet RPC receipt validation of BOTH transfer logs, same buyer and block confirmations.
- Operator-reviewed snapshots; independently public EIP-191 seller and treasury wallet ownership proof.
- No private keys or gas paid by marketplace; GitHub + Render static deployment.
- Unique tx-hash public revenue ledger with operator verification before counting commission.

## Conditions NOT yet met
1. A real operator-controlled Base receiving address has NOT yet been verified. Original VM1/VM5 wallets have unresolved backup/ownership caveats; do not assign treasury or route third-party funds to unbacked addresses.
2. Existing VM5 own demonstration listing has no wallet signature/proof and no independently agreed delivery/refund terms. Therefore paymentEnabled=false; external x402 purchases do not count as RelayMarket orders.
3. No evidence of real independent buyers or 10% commission receipts exists. Do not claim purchases.
4. Legal and operational readiness: user-controlled business identity, applicability of intermediary/payment regulations, tax/Sanctions/KYC where necessary, support, consumer protection, vendor liability, refunds, IP license transfer rights, abuse prevention, disaster recovery and dispute policy require real owner review.
5. A real buyer must explicitly confirm an irreversible non-escrow payment; no agent must automatically spend owner funds.
6. Atomic wallet support is not universal. Compatible wallet EIP-5792 on Base required. No fallback.
7. Before monetized launch: independent code/security review, threat model, real-world test transaction authorized by the wallet owner, receipt and revenue proof, automated seller license delivery or clear explicit SLA. The one-time merchant/cost consent must be informed.
8. Never deploy unaudited optional Solidity escrow/splitter to mainnet or sign any gas/USDC transaction on behalf of an owner without separate approval.

## Operator wallet onboarding
Use https://relaymarket-agents-vm5.onrender.com/operator.html to create a *public ownership signature* with no gas. Repo owner verifies it with scripts/configure-treasury.mjs and commits the public config. Do not publish a buyer checkout until all applicable gates have been checked. The signature can be generated by a safe personally controlled wallet without revealing a seed phrase.

## Why no onchain contract is necessary for compatible buyers
EIP-5792 atomicRequired=true can bundle two canonical USDC transfers in one smart wallet transaction, making both transfers succeed or both fail. Legacy externally developed smart contracts in contracts/ remain experimental and must not be deployed without separate audit/authorization.

## Revenue accounting
Only the real, independent buyer-paid Base onchain transfer pair with matching exact amount+seller+treasury can be recorded as a sale. Unique txHash is booked once in public/ledger.json. Seller fulfillment is independently established and cannot be inferred from a payment receipt.
