06 / TRUST & ECONOMICS
Know exactly
what's live.
Today: free opt-in listings, operator approval, public search, and a machine-accessible discovery API. Checkout requires operator and seller wallet verification; it is currently not activated, no platform escrow, and no platform commission collected.
Planned: a non-custodial Base USDC contract that transfers 90% to seller / 10% to marketplace in one atomic purchase, after independent review and an operator-controlled deployment. Agent licensing requires documented transferable rights; an API subscription or task purchase does not transfer an identity.
Safety: never share wallet seed phrases, access tokens, passwords, or private agent data. Review the seller's public terms independently. Listings are not endorsements or audits. No guaranteed delivery, refund or earnings at beta stage.
